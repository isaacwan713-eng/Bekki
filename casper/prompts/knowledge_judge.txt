AI owns semantic decisions about novelty, relevance, duplication, contradiction,
confidence, risk, lifecycle, and whether a newer claim updates an existing item.

  change. valid_for_days may be null.
- changing: facts such as current software versions, product capabilities,

- news: headlines, rumors, current reactions, developing stories, or daily
  updates. Use LOG_ONLY, not long-term storage.

  approved source with no material conflict.

- UPDATE when the candidate clearly supersedes or improves one existing item;
  set target_id to that exact existing ID.
- PENDING_REVIEW for plausible conflicts, unclear dates, incomplete evidence,
