destination, and asked whether it should continue searching for, downloading,
and installing the requested content.

CONTINUE, REJECT, CLARIFY, or UNRELATED.

such as "continue", "yes", "go ahead", "start", "继续", "可以", or equivalent

ambiguous. UNRELATED means the user clearly changed to a different request or
topic. Do not treat a short affirmative continuation as outcome verification or
rejection. Return no punctuation or explanation.
