Return one compact JSON object only. No Markdown or extra prose.

Decide whether this shopping turn is an exact-product purchase lookup or an
open-ended shopping request. You, not Python, own this semantic decision.

EXACT_PRODUCT means the user wants to find where one already-identified item
can be bought. The item may be named in the current request or referenced from
recent conversation, such as "the first one", "that model", or "第一个".
OPEN_ENDED means the user is still asking to browse, compare, or choose among a
category or feature set. UNRESOLVED_REFERENCE means the request clearly points
backward but the exact item cannot be identified reliably from the supplied
recent conversation.

Rules:
- For EXACT_PRODUCT, preserve the complete product identity: brand, model or
  line name, and differentiating words. Never reduce "Simple Modern Classic
  Tumbler" to "tumbler" or merely "Simple Modern".
- Resolve ordinals from the actual order in recent conversation. Do not guess
  an item that is absent.
- `source_scope` is CURRENT_REQUEST when the exact identity is stated now and
  RECENT_CONTEXT when it is resolved from prior conversation.
- `source_phrase` must be one literal, contiguous copy of the exact product
  name from that source. It must not be a paraphrase or a whole instruction.
- `resolved_title` should copy the same exact product name. Preserve brand and
  model spelling. Do not translate a model name.
- For EXACT_PRODUCT, write one to three natural US purchase searches. Every
  query must retain the complete exact product title. Good additions include
  buy, official, retailer, or where to buy. Do not add popularity, reviews,
  audience, size, color, material, or another feature unless it is part of the
  exact product name.
- The detected runtime profile supplies country, units, and Google/Bing. Do not
  reconsider the location or search engines.
- For OPEN_ENDED and UNRESOLVED_REFERENCE, leave title, brand, source phrase,
  and search queries empty.
