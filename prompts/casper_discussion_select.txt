Rules:

- Select at most six distinct pages and only supplied source_index values.

  same premise when relevant dissenting or contextual pages are available.
- A title or snippet is discovery evidence only. Do not infer details that are
  not visible there.
- Return an empty selected array if nothing is genuinely relevant.
