# Daily Knowledge Curator V1.10.2

Build: `bekki-daily-knowledge-curator-v1-10-2-20260828`

## What the V1.10.1 real-run log proved

The governed Desktop fallback returned a complete answer, the mixed-answer
partition stored two reusable claims, and the daily curator successfully
organized all 23 pending facts in bounded batches. The SNH48 records were also
placed in one broad ecosystem as intended.

The remaining error was lifecycle classification. The first partitioning AI
marked a present complete team list as permanent `stable` knowledge. Such an
active official set can change and should normally be `reviewable`, while a
durable conceptual distinction can remain stable.

## Repairs

- A separate Gemma 4 judgment independently audits every exact mixed-answer
  claim after partitioning. It owns the semantic choice between stable,
  reviewable, and current-turn-only lifecycles.
- Persistence now requires agreement from both AI judgments. Python validates
  the two structured contracts but contains no SNH48, team, organization, law,
  or other domain keyword classifier.
- The second auditor may shorten or add a proportional review interval. It may
  also reject persistence for current rosters, affiliations, schedules,
  prices, versions, events, or news.
- Invalid or missing lifecycle-audit output fails closed for persistence while
  leaving an otherwise complete external answer usable for the current turn.
- Mixed claims written by older builds are found and re-audited automatically
  during the next idle curator pass, even if that day's normal curation already
  completed. Lifecycle changes requeue the affected topic copy.
- The authoritative flat ledger retains an audit trail. Claims reclassified as
  current-only are expired and excluded from active recall rather than silently
  deleted.
- The persistence API rejects any new mixed claim that attempts to bypass the
  independent lifecycle audit.

Existing `.env`, `data`, memory, verified knowledge, topic files, and model
weights are preserved by the stable installer.
