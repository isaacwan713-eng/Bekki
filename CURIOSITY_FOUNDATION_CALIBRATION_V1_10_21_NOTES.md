# Bekki Curiosity Foundation Calibration V1.10.21

Build ID: `bekki-curiosity-foundation-calibration-v1-10-21-20260830`

This patch keeps new and developing public topics at an approachable level
after a foundation user turn.

- Topic maturity depends on distinct learned foundation coverage, not the raw
  number of related records.
- Drafted, asked, and unverified Curiosity records do not count as learned
  coverage.
- Several facts from one narrow organizational lane do not establish broad
  topic maturity.
- A FOUNDATION turn in NEW_OR_SPARSE or DEVELOPING must yield a FOUNDATION
  proposal.
- Question depth follows its hardest requested knowledge lens. Professional
  brand, audience, promotion, contract, governance, and operations analysis is
  SPECIALIST even when written as a comparison.
- The existing Selector independently reads the literal question and may
  dismiss a depth-disproportionate draft.
- No additional AI role, gate, model call, or Python keyword classifier was
  introduced.

Historical roster lifecycle and Knowledge routing behavior from V1.10.20 are
unchanged.
